src/make-app.ts

The composition root — where everything is wired

This is the single file responsible for assembling the entire application. Every factory function, every DAO adapter, every use case — they're all instantiated here and nowhere else. When you need to understand how a piece of the system connects to another, you start here.

It's intentionally the most boring file in the codebase: it creates things, passes them to each other, and does nothing else. No logic. No decisions. Just wiring.
